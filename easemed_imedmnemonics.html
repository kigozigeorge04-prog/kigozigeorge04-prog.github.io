Using this deck on the ward, not just for exams

Mnemonics are retrieval scaffolds They're built for rapid recall under pressure, not a substitute for clinical judgement. A few of these (SIRS, Ranson's) have been partially superseded by newer scoring systems (Sepsis-3/qSOFA, APACHE II) that sit alongside them — look for the legacy flag. Know both the mnemonic and which one is exam tradition vs current standard, especially before quoting one on a ward round.
