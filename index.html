What is EaseMed?

Your clinical method guide, in your pocket

EaseMed is a web app built specifically for Ugandan medical students and healthcare learners. It structures the clinical encounter from presenting complaint to management plan — the way Hutchison taught it.

📖

Based on Hutchison's Clinical Methods

Every section follows the Hutchison framework — the same textbook used in Ugandan medical schools.

🇺🇬

Uganda-specific clinical context

Red flags, treatment protocols, and EPI vaccine schedules are all tailored to Uganda Clinical Guidelines.

📶

Works offline — even in the ward

Built as a Progressive Web App. Save records locally when there's no internet. Sync when you're back online.
