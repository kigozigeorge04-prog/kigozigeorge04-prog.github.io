Purpose of the Platform

EaseMed is an educational clerking assistant built to help medical students and healthcare learners develop systematic, structured, and high-quality clinical documentation habits. All clerking prompts, examination checklists, and history-taking frameworks in EaseMed are grounded in Hutchison's Clinical Methods (25th Edition) — one of the most widely respected clinical medicine reference texts in use across East African medical schools.

The platform provides four core structured clerking modules, each tailored to a specific patient population and clinical context:

  • Internal Medicine — Full adult history-taking covering presenting complaint, history of presenting complaint (HPC), past medical history (PMH), drug and allergy history, social and family history, HIV context, review of systems, and comprehensive physical examination
  • Surgery — Structured surgical clerking covering presenting complaint, HPC, past surgical and medical history, review of systems, surgical physical examination, and preliminary assessment
  • Pediatrics — Child-focused clerking covering birth history, neonatal period, developmental milestones, immunisation status, growth monitoring, nutritional assessment, and red flag identification
  • Obstetrics & Gynaecology — Comprehensive women's health clerking covering menstrual history, obstetric history, gynaecological history, antenatal care (ANC) data, contraceptive history, and system-specific review of symptoms

EaseMed also provides an administrative module allowing supervisors and educators to track clerkship activity, monitor ward round readiness, and manage student accounts within their institution.

⚠️ Critical Limitation — Please Read Carefully EaseMed does not provide diagnoses, prescribe medications, recommend specific treatments, or make clinical decisions of any kind. The platform is a documentation and learning aid only. Any information entered into or generated by EaseMed must never be used as the sole basis for patient management. All clinical decisions must be made by a qualified, registered clinician following proper, direct patient assessment. If you are ever uncertain about a clinical situation, put down the app and speak to your supervising clinician immediately.

EaseMed does not replace hospital information systems, electronic medical records (EMRs), official clinical referral forms, or any documentation system mandated by your institution or the Ministry of Health Uganda.
