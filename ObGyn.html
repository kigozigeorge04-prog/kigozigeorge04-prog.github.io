🔴 PV Bleeding — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions to Ask
  • Amount: How many pads per day? Are you soaking through? Any clots (size)?
  • Duration: How many days has this been going on? Continuous or on and off?
  • Colour: Is it bright red, dark red, brown, or black?
  • Timing: Is it related to your periods? Between periods? After sex?
  • Pregnancy: Could you be pregnant? When was your last period?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Positive pregnancy test + PV bleed + pain → ECTOPIC PREGNANCY
  • Post-menopausal bleeding → ENDOMETRIAL CARCINOMA
  • Heavy bleeding + dizziness + pallor → haemorrhagic shock
🔴 Pelvic Pain — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions — SOCRATES
  • Site: Where exactly? Left, right, middle, both sides?
  • Onset: Sudden or gradual?
  • Character: Sharp, dull, crampy, colicky, or constant?
  • Radiation: To shoulder tip? Back? Thigh?
  • Severity: 0-10 scale? Stops normal activities?
  • Timing: Related to periods? Sex? Bowel movements?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Sudden severe unilateral pain + instability → RUPTURED ECTOPIC
  • Shoulder tip pain + peritonism → HAEMOPERITONEUM
  • Fever + bilateral pain + discharge → PID
🟡 Vaginal Discharge — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Colour: White? Yellow? Green? Grey? Blood-stained?
  • Smell: Fishy? Offensive? Any odour?
  • Amount: Light, moderate, or heavy?
  • Itch: Any itching? Burning? Soreness?
  • Timing: Related to cycle? Sex? Antibiotics?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Blood-stained offensive discharge + weight loss → MALIGNANCY
  • Mucopurulent discharge + pelvic pain + fever → PID
  • Post-menopausal discharge → endometrial carcinoma
🔵 Amenorrhoea — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Primary vs secondary: Have you ever had a period?
  • Duration: How many months/cycles missed?
  • Pregnancy: Any chance? Done a test?
  • Lifestyle: Stress? Weight loss? Excessive exercise?
  • Hormonal: Hair growth? Acne? Hot flushes? Galactorrhoea?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Positive pregnancy test → MANAGE AS PREGNANCY
  • Cyclical pain + no periods → HAEMATOCOLPOS
  • Galactorrhoea + amenorrhoea → PROLACTINOMA
🟢 Pelvic Mass — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Discovery: When and how noticed?
  • Growth: Growing? Slowly or rapidly?
  • Pain: Any pain? Sudden pain (think torsion)?
  • Pressure: Difficulty passing urine? Constipation?
  • Systemic: Weight loss? Loss of appetite?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Rapid growth + weight loss + ascites → OVARIAN MALIGNANCY
  • Post-menopausal mass → CARCINOMA
  • Sudden pain in known mass → TORSION
🟣 Infertility — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Duration: How long trying to conceive?
  • Type: Primary or secondary?
  • Menstrual history: Regular cycles? Painful periods?
  • STI history: PID, gonorrhoea, chlamydia?
  • Partner: Been investigated?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Infertility + galactorrhoea → PROLACTINOMA
  • Age >35 with >6 months infertility → EARLY INVESTIGATION
🟤 Prolapse — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • What comes out: Can you describe it?
  • When: Coughing, sneezing, lifting, standing?
  • Reducibility: Does it go back on its own?
  • Urinary: Leaking urine? Difficulty emptying?
  • Bowel: Difficulty passing stool?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Irreducible / ulcerated prolapse → URGENT SURGICAL REVIEW
  • Urinary retention → CATHETERISE
🔴 Post-Coital Bleeding — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Amount: Spot or enough for a pad?
  • Frequency: Every time? How long?
  • Discharge: Any odour? Colour change?
  • Pain: During or after sex?
  • Smear: When was your last Pap smear?
🚨 Red Flags
  • PCB + offensive discharge + weight loss → CERVICAL CARCINOMA
  • PCB + abnormal smear → URGENT COLPOSCOPY
🔴 Post-Menopausal Bleeding — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Definition: Any bleeding >12 months after last period
  • Amount: Spotting or heavy?
  • Duration: One episode or multiple?
  • Associated: Pelvic pain? Weight loss?
  • HRT: On hormone replacement therapy?
🚨 Red Flags
  • ANY post-menopausal bleeding → ENDOMETRIAL CARCINOMA
  • Associated pelvic mass → UTERINE/OVARIAN MALIGNANCY
🟠 Vulval Itch — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Duration: Acute or chronic?
  • Severity: Keeping you awake at night?
  • Discharge: White curdy? Grey fishy? Yellow-green?
  • Skin changes: Redness? White patches? Ulcers?
  • Diabetes / HIV: Any history?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Chronic itch + white thickened skin → LICHEN SCLEROSUS / VIN / CARCINOMA
  • Vulval ulcer + lymphadenopathy → SYPHILIS / CARCINOMA
🔵 Urinary Symptoms — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Stress incontinence: Leak when coughing, sneezing?
  • Urge incontinence: Sudden urge + leak?
  • Frequency: How often? Waking at night?
  • Dysuria: Pain/burning when passing urine?
  • Fistula: Continuous PV leaking?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Continuous PV leaking → VESICOVAGINAL FISTULA
  • Urinary retention → CATHETERISE
  • Haematuria + weight loss → RENAL MALIGNANCY
🔴 Labour Pains — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Gestational age: How many weeks?
  • Contractions: How often? Getting stronger?
  • Show: Any blood-stained mucus?
  • ROM: Any liquor draining? Colour?
  • Previous CS: Any previous caesarean sections?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Preterm labour (<37 wks) → TOCOLYSIS, STEROIDS
  • Previous CS + strong contractions → SCAR RUPTURE
  • Meconium-stained liquor → FETAL DISTRESS
🔴 APH — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Amount: Spotting, moderate, or heavy?
  • Pain: Painful (abruption) or painless (previa)?
  • Placenta: Any previous scan showing low-lying placenta?
  • Fetal movement: Still feeling baby?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Painless APH → PLACENTA PRAEVIA — DO NOT EXAMINE VAGINALLY
  • Painful APH + rigid abdomen → PLACENTAL ABRUPTION
  • Haemodynamic instability → RESUSCITATE
🟡 Reduced Fetal Movement — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Normal pattern: What is normal for your baby?
  • Duration: How long has movement been reduced?
  • Kick counting: <10 movements in 12 hours is concerning
  • Risk factors: Diabetes? Hypertension? IUGR?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Absent fetal movement → URGENT CTG / USS
  • RFM + no fetal heart → INTRAUTERINE FETAL DEATH
💧 Rupture of Membranes — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Time: When did your waters break?
  • Colour: Clear? Green/meconium? Offensive?
  • Amount: Gush or trickle?
  • Gestational age: Term or preterm?
🚨 Red Flags
  • ROM + cord felt → CORD PROLAPSE (emergency)
  • Offensive liquor + fever → CHORIOAMNIONITIS
  • PPROM <34 wks → STEROIDS + ANTIBIOTICS
🔴 Hypertension / Pre-eclampsia — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Headache: Frontal/occipital? Severe? Persistent?
  • Visual: Blurring? Flashing lights?
  • Epigastric pain: RUQ pain (HELLP)?
  • Swelling: Sudden facial/hand oedema?
  • Fits: Any convulsions?
🚨 Red Flags — Severe Features
  • Fits → ECLAMPSIA (MgSO₄ immediately)
  • BP ≥160/110 + symptoms → SEVERE PRE-ECLAMPSIA
  • Epigastric pain + ↑LFTs + thrombocytopaenia → HELLP
🟡 Nausea / Vomiting — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Severity: Can you keep food/fluids down?
  • Frequency: How many times per day?
  • Weight loss: How much?
  • Dehydration: Dry mouth? Reduced urine?
  • Jaundice: Yellowing of skin/eyes?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Inability to keep anything down + weight loss → HYPEREMESIS
  • Vomiting + jaundice → ACUTE FATTY LIVER / HEPATITIS
  • Vomiting + severe headache + hypertension → PRE-ECLAMPSIA
🔴 Abdominal Pain in Pregnancy — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Character: Constant or intermittent?
  • Site: Lower abdo, RIF, RUQ, loin?
  • PV bleeding: Any bleeding?
  • Fever: Any fever?
  • Previous CS: Scar tenderness?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Constant severe pain + rigid uterus + bleeding → ABRUPTION
  • Scar tenderness + fetal distress → UTERINE RUPTURE
  • RIF pain + fever → APPENDICITIS
🔵 Urinary Symptoms in Pregnancy — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Dysuria: Burning on micturition?
  • Loin pain + fever: Pyelonephritis?
  • Frequency: More than usual?
  • Haematuria: Blood in urine?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Fever + loin pain + rigors → PYELONEPHRITIS
  • Asymptomatic bacteriuria → TREAT
🔴 PPH — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Timing: Primary (<24 hrs) or secondary (24 hrs–6 weeks)?
  • Amount: How much blood loss?
  • 4 Ts: Tone, Trauma, Tissue, Thrombin?
  • Placenta: Delivered completely?
🚨 Red Flags
  • PPH + haemodynamic collapse → MASSIVE HAEMORRHAGE PROTOCOL
  • Atony not responding → MISOPROSTOL, CARBOPROST
🟠 Fever / Sepsis — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Malaria: Always screen in Uganda
  • Chorioamnionitis: Fever + offensive liquor
  • Endometritis: Fever + offensive lochia
  • Pyelonephritis: Loin pain + fever + rigors
🚨 Red Flags
  • Malaria → IV ARTESUNATE if severe
  • Septic shock → SEPSIS-6 BUNDLE
  • Chorioamnionitis → DELIVER
🟢 Routine ANC Visit — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • ANC visits: How many? Uganda MoH recommends 8
  • Fetal movement: Feeling baby move well?
  • Danger signs: Headache? Visual changes? Bleeding? RFM?
  • Medications: Iron? Folic acid? IPTp?
  • HIV: On PMTCT? Adherence?
🚨 Danger Signs
  • Severe headache, visual changes → PRE-ECLAMPSIA
  • PV bleeding → APH
  • Reduced fetal movement → FETAL DISTRESS
  • Fever → MALARIA / INFECTION
⚠️ Preterm Labor — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Gestational age: Between 24+0 and 36+6 weeks
  • Contractions: Regular? Painful?
  • Risk factors: Previous preterm birth? Multiple pregnancy? UTI?
  • Infection: Any fever? Offensive discharge?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Cervical dilatation ≥3 cm → ESTABLISH LABOUR CARE
  • Preterm labour + infection → EXPEDITE DELIVERY
  • Steroids if <34 wks → ADMINISTER
⚠️ Preeclampsia — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • BP: ≥140/90 mmHg
  • Severe features: BP ≥160/110, headache, visual disturbance
  • Previous: PET in previous pregnancy?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Eclampsia → MgSO₄ 4g IV BOLUS
  • HELLP → URGENT DELIVERY
🔴 Placental Abruption / Previa — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Abruption: Painful bleeding, tender uterus
  • Previa: Painless bleeding — DO NOT examine vaginally
  • Risk factors: Previous CS, smoking, hypertension
🚨 Red Flags
  • Abruption with fetal distress → EMERGENCY CS
  • Previa with heavy bleeding → EMERGENCY CS
  • DIC in abruption → CHECK PLATELETS, FIBRINOGEN
🔴 Ectopic Pregnancy — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Risk factors: Previous ectopic, PID, IUD use
  • Classic triad: Amenorrhoea, pain, bleeding
  • Shoulder tip: Diaphragmatic irritation
  • Collapse: Any fainting? Dizziness?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Ruptured ectopic → EMERGENCY SURGERY
  • Haemodynamically unstable → DO NOT WAIT FOR TESTS
🟣 Molar Pregnancy — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Classic: Bleeding, uterus larger than dates
  • Hyperemesis: Very high βHCG
  • USS: 'Snowstorm' appearance
🚨 Red Flags
  • Excessive bleeding → URGENT EVACUATION
  • Persistent βHCG → FOLLOW-UP FOR GTD
🟡 Gestational Diabetes — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Risk factors: BMI >30, previous GDM, family history
  • OGTT: Fasting ≥5.1, 2-hr ≥8.5 mmol/L
  • Management: Diet, exercise; metformin or insulin
🚨 Red Flags
  • Polyhydramnios + large for dates → CHECK GDM
  • Poorly controlled GDM → RISK OF STILLBIRTH
🟠 Obstetric Cholestasis — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Itching: Generalised (palms and soles) without rash
  • LFTs: Elevated bile acids and transaminases
  • Treatment: Ursodeoxycholic acid
🚨 Red Flags
  • Bile acids >40 μmol/L → INCREASED STILLBIRTH RISK
  • Plan induction at 37–38 weeks
🧠 Perinatal Mental Health — Clinical Guide
📋 Key Questions
  • Depression: Low mood, loss of interest, sleep disturbance
  • Anxiety: Excessive worry about baby, panic attacks
  • Psychosis: Hallucinations, delusions (emergency)
  • Suicidal thoughts: Thoughts of harming self or baby?
🚨 Red Flags
  • Suicidal ideation → IMMEDIATE PSYCHIATRIC REFERRAL
  • Psychosis → URGENT PERINATAL PSYCHIATRY
  • Thoughts of harming baby → EMERGENCY ASSESSMENT
💡 General Clinical Prompts
📋 Always Remember
  • Always establish LMP, pregnancy status, and obstetric history
  • For pain: use SOCRATES
  • In Uganda: always screen for malaria
  • Red flags are medical emergencies — don't delay referral
