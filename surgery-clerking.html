❤️ Cardiovascular Examination
▼
🔍 Inspection
Look for: JVP, scars, visible pulsations, chest wall deformity, peripheral oedema, cyanosis, clubbing.
Findings (click to toggle): Elevated JVP 💡Right heart failure, fluid overload, tamponade — measure JVP height Praecordial scar 💡Previous cardiac surgery — sternotomy or thoracotomy Visible pulsations 💡Aneurysm, high-output state, cardiac hypertrophy Peripheral oedema 💡Heart failure, venous insufficiency, renal disease Clubbing 💡Lung cancer, cyanotic heart disease, IBD, liver cirrhosis Central cyanosis 💡Cyanotic heart disease, severe lung disease — SpO2 < 85%
🖐 Palpation
Feel for: Apex beat (position, character), heaves, thrills, peripheral pulses (rate, rhythm, volume).
Findings (click to toggle): Apex beat displaced 💡Cardiomegaly — LV enlargement LV heave 💡Left ventricular hypertrophy — hypertension, aortic stenosis RV heave 💡Right ventricular hypertrophy — pulmonary hypertension, pulmonary stenosis Thrill 💡Palpable murmur — significant stenosis or regurgitation Tapping apex 💡Mitral stenosis — palpable S1 Weak pulses 💡Peripheral vascular disease, low cardiac output Bounding pulses 💡Aortic regurgitation, PDA, high-output state Capillary refill >2s 💡Shock, hypovolaemia, peripheral vasoconstriction
👂 Auscultation
Listen for: Heart sounds, added sounds, murmurs (timing, location, radiation, intensity).
Findings (click to toggle): S3 gallop 💡Heart failure, volume overload — 'ventricular gallop' S4 💡Stiff ventricle — hypertension, aortic stenosis, ischaemia Ejection systolic murmur 💡Aortic stenosis, pulmonary stenosis — listen for radiation Pansystolic murmur 💡Mitral regurgitation, VSD — radiates to axilla Early diastolic murmur 💡Aortic regurgitation — best heard at left sternal edge Mid-diastolic murmur 💡Mitral stenosis — opening snap may be present Pericardial rub 💡Pericarditis — scratchy sound, changes with position Irregular rhythm 💡Atrial fibrillation, ectopics — assess rate and symptoms
🫁 Respiratory Examination
▼
🔍 Inspection
Look for: Respiratory rate, accessory muscle use, tracheal position, chest symmetry, scars, deformities, cyanosis.
Findings (click to toggle): Tachypnoea 💡Pneumonia, asthma, acidosis, pulmonary embolism — count RR Accessory muscle use 💡Respiratory distress — COPD, asthma, severe pneumonia Chest indrawing 💡Lower airway obstruction — pneumonia, bronchiolitis Nasal flaring 💡Respiratory distress — children and adults Tracheal deviation 💡Pneumothorax, pleural effusion, lung collapse Barrel chest 💡COPD, asthma — hyperinflation Kyphoscoliosis 💡Restrictive lung disease — may cause respiratory failure Pursed-lip breathing 💡COPD, asthma — maintaining airway pressure
🖐 Palpation
Feel for: Tracheal position, chest expansion, tactile fremitus, tenderness.
Findings (click to toggle): Reduced expansion (R) 💡Pneumothorax, effusion, collapse — right side Reduced expansion (L) 💡Pneumothorax, effusion, collapse — left side Tactile fremitus increased 💡Consolidation — lobar pneumonia Tactile fremitus reduced 💡Effusion, pneumothorax, fibrosis
👂 Percussion
Listen for: Resonance, dullness, hyperresonance, stony dullness.
Findings (click to toggle): Dull (R base) 💡Pleural effusion — right side Dull (L base) 💡Pleural effusion — left side Stony dull 💡Massive pleural effusion — likely malignant Hyperresonant 💡Pneumothorax, bullous disease
👂 Auscultation
Listen for: Breath sounds, added sounds – crackles, wheeze, rhonchi, pleural rub.
Findings (click to toggle): Coarse crackles 💡Secretions, bronchiectasis, pulmonary oedema Fine crackles 💡Fibrosis, early oedema, interstitial lung disease Bilateral basal crackles 💡Pulmonary oedema — consider heart failure Expiratory wheeze 💡Asthma, COPD — lower airway narrowing Bronchial breathing 💡Consolidation — pneumonia above a patent airway Reduced breath sounds 💡Effusion, pneumothorax, collapse Pleural rub 💡Pleurisy, pulmonary infarction — scratchy, often painful
🫃 Abdominal Examination
▼
🔍 Inspection
Look for: Shape, symmetry, scars, striae, veins (caput medusae), visible peristalsis, hernias, distension, masses.
Findings (click to toggle): Distended 💡Ascites, obstruction, organomegaly Scaphoid 💡Malnutrition, wasting — inspect for signs Visible peristalsis 💡Intestinal obstruction — often pyloric stenosis in infants Caput medusae 💡Portal hypertension — liver cirrhosis Surgical scar 💡Previous laparotomy — note position and type Umbilical hernia 💡Common in children — usually reducible
👂 Auscultation
Listen for: Bowel sounds (normal, absent, hyperactive, tinkling), bruits.
Findings (click to toggle): Bowel sounds absent 💡Ileus, peritonitis, late obstruction Hyperactive bowel sounds 💡Early obstruction, gastroenteritis Tinkling bowel sounds 💡Mechanical obstruction — high-pitched Aortic bruit 💡Aortic stenosis, aneurysm — listen in epigastrium Renal bruit 💡Renal artery stenosis — listen in flank
🖐 Palpation
Feel for: Tenderness, guarding, rigidity, masses, organomegaly, hernia orifices, cough impulse.
Findings (click to toggle): Generalised tenderness 💡Peritonitis, gastroenteritis — assess severity RIF tenderness 💡Appendicitis — McBurney's point, rebound RUQ tenderness 💡Cholecystitis, hepatitis — Murphy's sign Guarding 💡Peritoneal irritation — surgical abdomen Rigidity 💡Peritonitis — board-like abdomen, emergency Rebound tenderness 💡Peritoneal inflammation — appendicitis, peritonitis Hepatomegaly 💡Hepatitis, congestion, malignancy — measure span Splenomegaly 💡Portal hypertension, malaria, haematological disease Palpable mass 💡Tumour, cyst, inflammatory mass — assess location, size, consistency
👂 Percussion
Listen for: Dullness (liver span, splenic dullness), shifting dullness (ascites), tympany.
Findings (click to toggle): Shifting dullness 💡Ascites — liver, cardiac, malignant causes Liver span increased 💡Hepatomegaly — measure in cm Tympany 💡Bowel obstruction, ileus — hyperresonant
💧 Genitourinary Examination
▼
🔍 Inspection
Look for: External genitalia – penis, urethral meatus, scrotum, testicles, skin changes, swelling, discharge.
Findings (click to toggle): Scrotal swelling 💡Hydrocele, hernia, tumour, torsion — assess transillumination Urethral discharge 💡STI, urethritis — send for Gram stain and culture Skin lesions (penis/scrotum) 💡STI, malignancy, infection — biopsy if suspected
🖐 Palpation
Feel for: Testicular size, consistency, tenderness, masses, epididymis, spermatic cord, hernias.
Findings (click to toggle): Tender testis 💡Epididymitis, torsion, trauma — urgent Doppler Testicular mass 💡Testicular cancer — ultrasound and tumour markers Inguinal hernia 💡Assess reducibility, cough impulse, risk of incarceration
🖐 Digital Rectal Examination (DRE)
Assess: Sphincter tone, prostate (males) – size, consistency, nodules, tenderness; rectal masses, blood on glove, stool colour/consistency.
Findings (click to toggle): Enlarged prostate 💡BPH, cancer — record approximate size Nodular prostate 💡Prostate cancer — consider PSA, biopsy Rectal mass 💡Rectal carcinoma — urgent colonoscopy Blood on glove 💡Lower GI bleed — consider malignancy, haemorrhoids, diverticular disease Reduced sphincter tone 💡Cauda equina, spinal cord lesion, previous surgery

🔍 Inspection & Mental Status
Assess: Consciousness, speech, pupils, posture, facial asymmetry, involuntary movements.
Findings (click to toggle): Confused/drowsy 💡Metabolic, toxic, CNS infection, raised ICP Facial asymmetry 💡Cranial nerve VII palsy, stroke — differentiate UMN/LMN Ptosis 💡CN III palsy, Horner's, myasthenia gravis Abnormal pupillary response 💡CN II/III lesion, raised ICP — check both eyes
🖐 Motor, Sensory & Reflexes
Test: Power (MRC grade), tone, coordination, sensation, reflexes (biceps, triceps, knee, ankle, plantar).
Findings (click to toggle): Power reduced (R) 💡UMN or LMN lesion — grade on MRC scale Power reduced (L) 💡UMN or LMN lesion — grade on MRC scale Increased tone 💡UMN lesion — spasticity, rigidity Decreased tone 💡LMN lesion — flaccidity, hypotonia Babinski positive 💡UMN lesion — corticospinal tract Clonus 💡UMN lesion — sustained clonus = upper motor neuron Sensory level 💡Spinal cord lesion — define level
🖐 Cranial Nerves
Test: CN I–XII as clinically indicated — vision, EOMs, facial sensation/movement, hearing, swallow, tongue.
Findings (click to toggle): CN deficit (specify) 💡Cranial nerve palsy — localise lesion Visual field defect 💡Optic chiasm or optic nerve lesion
🦴 Musculoskeletal Examination
▼
🔍 Inspection
Look for: Deformity, swelling, discolouration, muscle wasting, limb length discrepancy, skin changes.
Findings (click to toggle): Deformity 💡Fracture, arthritis, congenital — describe angulation Swelling 💡Effusion, soft tissue, bony — assess size and consistency Muscle wasting 💡Neuropathy, myopathy, disuse — measure circumference Skin changes 💡Ulcer, pigmentation, scar — note location
🖐 Palpation
Feel for: Tenderness, warmth, crepitus, joint effusion, bone alignment, peripheral pulses, capillary refill, sensation distal.
Findings (click to toggle): Tenderness 💡Fracture, infection, inflammation — localise Crepitus 💡Osteoarthritis, loose body, fracture — feel and listen Joint effusion 💡Inflammatory, traumatic, septic — assess bulge sign Warmth 💡Infection, inflammation — compare to contralateral
🔄 Range of Motion
Assess: Active and passive ROM; pain, restriction, instability.
Findings (click to toggle): Restricted ROM 💡Arthritis, effusion, contracture — measure degrees Pain on movement 💡Inflammatory, traumatic, septic — localise Joint instability 💡Ligament injury — assess for laxity
🩹 Wound / Ulcer Examination
▼
🔍 Inspection
Look for: Site, size (cm), edges (sloping, punched-out, undermined, everted, rolled), base (slough, granulation, necrotic, exposed bone/tendon), discharge (serous, purulent, haemorrhagic), surrounding skin.
Findings (click to toggle): Necrotic base 💡Ischaemia, infection, pressure — consider debridement Purulent discharge 💡Infection — send for culture and sensitivity Everted edges 💡Squamous cell carcinoma (Marjolin's) — biopsy Undermined edges 💡Tuberculosis, Buruli ulcer — probe for sinus Granulation tissue 💡Healing wound — moist, pink-red
🖐 Palpation
Feel for: Tenderness, temperature, subcutaneous emphysema, deep extension, sinus tracts, regional lymphadenopathy, distal pulses.
Findings (click to toggle): Subcutaneous emphysema 💡Necrotising fasciitis, gas gangrene — emergency Regional lymphadenopathy 💡Infection, malignancy — assess nodes Palpable sinus 💡Chronic infection, osteomyelitis — probe
➕ Lymphatics, Skin, Breast, Neck
▼
🔍 Inspection & Palpation
Look and feel for: Lymph nodes: cervical, axillary, inguinal – size, consistency, tenderness, mobility. Skin: rashes, pigmentation, scars, pressure areas. Breast: symmetry, peau d'orange, nipple retraction, quadrants, axillary tail. Thyroid: size, consistency, nodules, bruit.
Findings (click to toggle): Cervical lymphadenopathy 💡Infection (URTI, TB), lymphoma, malignancy — assess size and consistency Axillary lymphadenopathy 💡Breast cancer, lymphoma, infection — unilateral vs bilateral Inguinal lymphadenopathy 💡STI, lower limb infection, lymphoma, malignancy Breast lump 💡Fibroadenoma, cyst, carcinoma — assess size, mobility, skin changes Peau d'orange 💡Inflammatory breast cancer — urgent referral Thyroid nodule 💡Thyroid cancer, adenoma, goitre — ultrasound and FNA Skin rash 💡Allergy, infection, drug reaction — describe morphology
